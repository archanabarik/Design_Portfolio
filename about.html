About me

I'm Archana Kumari, a Lead Product Designer focused on building scalable digital products and the teams behind them.
‍
My design principles are rooted in reframing problems before solving them, designing decisions that carry the weight so people don't have to, and owning the whole system — not just the surface people see. I bring structure to ambiguity, hold design accountable to business outcomes, and turn complex, cross-functional problems into experiences that feel simple and inevitable.
‍
I care about my work, but I care just as much about the people building it alongside me and the people behind the screen using it.

Now

Shaping direction

I connect what users need with where the business is headed — and make sure the systems underneath can actually hold that up as things scale.

Building the foundation

The interface is the last thing I design, not the first. From Box8 to Acko, I've built the structural systems that everything else stands on.

Working across the whole system

A product breaks at its weakest link, not its best screen. I design for customers, crews, and internal teams alike — sitting with operations early, not discovering their constraints late.

Designing with AI, not through it

AI speeds up how I research, prototype, and iterate — but the judgment, the taste, and the accountability stay mine.

Growing other designers

At Growth School, I mentor early-career designers — passing on the things I had to learn the hard way.
